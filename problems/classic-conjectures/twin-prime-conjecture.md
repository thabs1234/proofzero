# Twin Prime Conjecture

**Status:** Open
**Field:** Number Theory
**First stated:** 1849 (Alphonse de Polignac)

## Statement

There are infinitely many primes p such that p+2 is also prime.

## Progress

- 1849 — de Polignac generalizes
- 1919 — Brun: sum of reciprocals of twin primes converges (Brun's constant)
- 2013 — Zhang Yitang: bounded gaps (≤ 70,000,000)
- 2014 — Polymath8: bound reduced to 246
- 2014 — Maynard: infinitely many bounded gaps

## Notes

Still no proof of gap = 2. Zhang's breakthrough was the first finite bound.
