# Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture

**Status:** Open
**Field:** Arithmetic Geometry / Elliptic Curves
**Prize:** $1,000,000 (Clay Millennium)

## Statement

For an elliptic curve E over ℚ, the rank of E(ℚ) equals the order of vanishing of L(E,s) at s=1.

## Progress

- 1960s — Birch & Swinnerton-Dyer formulate conjecture (numerical)
- 1977 — Coates-Wiles: if L(E,1)≠0 then E(ℚ) is finite
- 1983 — Gross-Zagier: if L(E,1)=0 is a simple zero, rank ≥ 1
- 1999 — Kolyvagin: rank 0 and 1 cases resolved
- 2024 — Full conjecture still open (rank ≥ 2)
