# Hodge Conjecture

**Status:** Open
**Field:** Algebraic Geometry
**Prize:** $1,000,000 (Clay Millennium)

## Statement

On a non-singular projective algebraic variety over ℂ, every Hodge class is a rational linear combination of classes of algebraic cycles.

## Progress

- 1950 — Hodge formulates conjecture
- 1960s — Lefschetz (1,1)-theorem: Hodge classes from divisors
- No general proof; known only for specific cases
