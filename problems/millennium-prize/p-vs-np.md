# P vs NP

**Status:** Open
**Field:** Computational Complexity Theory
**First stated:** 1971 (Stephen Cook)
**Prize:** $1,000,000 (Clay Millennium)

## Statement

Is every problem whose solution can be verified in polynomial time (NP) also solvable in polynomial time (P)?

## Progress

- 1971 — Cook defines NP-completeness; Cook-Levin theorem
- 1972 — Karp shows 21 NP-complete problems
- 1976 — Baker, Gill, Solovay: relativization barrier (oracle results don't resolve)
- 1979 — Tardos example for natural proofs barrier
- 1986 — Razborov & Rudich: natural proofs barrier (if one-way functions exist)
- 1990s — Geometric complexity theory (Mulmuley)
- 2010s — Ryan Williams: NEXP ⊄ ACC⁰
- 2024 — No major breakthroughs; most experts believe P ≠ NP

## Notes

If P=NP: all NP problems have efficient algorithms (cryptography breaks).
If P≠NP: no efficient general algorithm for NP-complete problems.
