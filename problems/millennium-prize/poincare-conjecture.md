# Poincaré Conjecture

**Status:** SOLVED (2003)
**Field:** Topology / Geometric Analysis
**First stated:** 1904 (Henri Poincaré)
**Prize:** $1,000,000 (Clay Millennium) — claimed by Perelman, declined

## Statement

Every simply connected, closed 3-manifold is homeomorphic to the 3-sphere.

## Proof

- 2002–2003 — Grigori Perelman posts three preprints on arXiv
- Uses Ricci flow with surgery (Hamilton's program)
- 2006 — Cao & Zhu, Morgan & Tian publish complete expositions
- 2010 — Clay Institute awards prize; Perelman declines
- 2010 — Fields Medal offered; Perelman declines

## Notes

Only Millennium problem solved to date. Perelman's proof remains the gold standard for rigor.
