# Yang-Mills Mass Gap

**Status:** Open
**Field:** Quantum Field Theory / Mathematical Physics
**Prize:** $1,000,000 (Clay Millennium)

## Statement

Prove that for any compact simple gauge group, Yang-Mills theory on ℝ⁴ has a mass gap Δ > 0.

## Progress

- 1954 — Yang & Mills introduce non-abelian gauge theory
- 1960s — 't Hooft proves renormalizability
- 1970s — Asymptotic freedom (Gross, Wilczek, Politzer)
- Lattice QCD simulations confirm mass gap numerically
- No rigorous mathematical proof
